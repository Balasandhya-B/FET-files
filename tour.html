OOTY

Ooty, officially known as Udhagamandalam, is a popular hill station in Tamil Nadu, India, famous for its cool weather, rolling tea gardens, and scenic mountain views.

  • Nilgiri Mountain Railway
  • Doddabetta Peak
  • Ooty Lake
  • Botanical Gardens
  • Pykara Lake and Falls
