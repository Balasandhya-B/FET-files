FOREST



A forest is a complex ecosystem dominated by trees and other woody vegetation. They are highly biodiverse environments that host a significant percentage of the world's land-dwelling species and play a vital role in climate regulation, soil protection, and carbon storage

Forests cover nearly one-third of the land area on our planet and are home to most of the world’s diversity of life on land. They are also essential to human health, purifying our water and air and serving as our first line of defense against new infectious diseases.
